    AirWater is allowed to say no

    Automatic refusal logic, not just disclaimer text.

    Appropriate use

    AirWater can

    • Compare candidate MOFs under the same assumptions.
    • Evaluate climate/material compatibility.
    • Estimate a research-screening yield range.
    • Prioritize materials for laboratory testing.
    • Generate an explainable operating schedule.
    • Identify the failure conditions behind a refusal.
    Outside scope

    AirWater cannot

    • Manufacture, characterize, or certify a MOF.
    • Guarantee device-scale water output.
    • Prove long-term material stability.
    • Rule out chemical leaching.
    • Declare harvested water potable.
    • Replace engineering or regulatory review.
    Recommended validation path — current position highlighted
    Research screening→ Curated experimental isotherms→ Lab adsorption validation→ Cycling + leaching→ Device prototype→ Water-quality testing→ Validated deployment model
