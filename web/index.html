      How the AI pipeline works

      The numerical result comes from the uptake model and ranking logic.

      1Read climateHourly humidity, temperature, and solar availability.
      2Predict uptakeEstimate water adsorption from climate and material descriptors.
      3Rank candidatesBalance yield, evidence, stability, cost proxy, and heat limits.
      4Plan operationChoose capture and release windows for the energy source.
      The packaged model uses synthetic demonstration rows generated from simplified material curves. Replace them with curated experimental isotherms before making scientific performance claims.
      Guardrails

      What this application can and cannot claim

      It is a research decision-support tool, not a certified water system.

      Appropriate use

      The app can

      • Compare candidate MOFs under the same assumptions.
      • Show how climate changes material selection.
      • Estimate a research-scale yield range.
      • Prioritize materials for laboratory testing.
      • Generate an explainable operating concept.
      Outside scope

      The app cannot

      • Manufacture, characterize, or certify a MOF.
      • Guarantee device-scale water output.
      • Rule out material degradation or chemical leaching.
      • Declare harvested water potable.
      • Replace engineering or regulatory review.
      Recommended validation path
      Curated data→Lab adsorption→Cycling and leaching→Device prototype→Water-quality testing
